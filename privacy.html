Privacy

Leadvault shows businesses the leads that come to them: form submissions, calls, contacts, deals and bookings. This page explains what the Leadvault website snippet collects and how the dashboard keeps it private.

What the website snippet sends

  • The fields of a form you submit on a business’s website, so the business can reply to you. Passwords, card numbers and similar fields are never read.
  • That you tapped a phone number on the site, and which number.
  • How you arrived at the site (for example a Google search, an ad, or an AI assistant), and the page you were on.

The snippet sets no cookies and doesn’t follow you to other websites. It remembers how you arrived in your own browser, on that site only.

Who can see it

Only the business you contacted and the agency team that manages its dashboard. Every dashboard needs a personal sign-in, and people are signed out after 5 minutes without activity.

Questions or removal

Contact the business you sent the form to. They can remove your details from their dashboard and their CRM.
